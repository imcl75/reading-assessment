## 18-7 Beatrix Potter and Peter Rabbit
- Born in London 1866 (28th of July): https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/discover/history/people/beatrix-potter
- Kept mice, rabbits, newts, birds (and caterpillars) in her schoolroom; drew animals: same National Trust page
- Pet rabbit Peter Piper inspired the story; illustrated letter to Noel Moore, September 1893, four rabbits incl. Peter: https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/peter-rabbit-the-tale-of-the-tale
- Six publishers rejected it; 250 copies self-published 16th of December 1901: V&A page above; National Trust page
- Frederick Warne published in 1902: V&A page; National Trust page
- Hill Top Farm bought 1905 with book profits, Lake District (Near Sawrey): National Trust page; https://beatrixpottersociety.org.uk/beatrix-potter/the-farmer/
- Left about 4,000 acres and (14-15) farms to the National Trust: National Trust page; Beatrix Potter Society page

## 19-7 Running for Gold in Athens
- Born 19th of April 1970, Pembury, Kent; joined army aged 18; army judo champion: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Holmes ; Britannica/Olympics.com summary via search (born Pembury, joined at 18)
- 800m final 23rd of August 2004, time 1:56.38: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_2004_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_800_metres
- 1500m gold 28th of August 2004: Wikipedia Kelly Holmes page; https://worldathletics.org/news/news/double-glory-for-kelly-holmes (five nights after the 800m)
- Aged 34; third woman ever to win the 800m/1500m double (Kazankina 1976, Masterkova 1996): search summary of World Athletics/Wikipedia
- Made a Dame (announced end of 2004, title held 2005): Wikipedia; date left out deliberately (sources differ: 2004 vs 2005)
- Track laps (800m = 2 laps, 1500m = 3.75) from standard 400m track.

## 20-7 Stevie Wonder, Musician
- Born 13th of May 1950, Saginaw, Michigan; blind since baby; raised in Detroit: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevie_Wonder ; https://www.britannica.com/biography/Stevie-Wonder (via search summary)
- Piano, harmonica, drums; skilled musician by age eight: Britannica summary above
- Joined Motown as a boy, named Little Stevie Wonder: Britannica (age 12) vs Wikipedia (age 11) differ, so age left out
- "Fingertips" 1963, aged 13, live recording, number one on Billboard, youngest artist to top chart: Wikipedia; https://www.thecurrent.org/feature/2017/06/14/today-in-music-history-stevie-wonder-enters-the-charts-at-age-13
- Talking Book 1972, Innervisions 1973, Songs in the Key of Life 1976: Wikipedia
- 25 Grammys and an Academy Award: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/watch-live-president-obama-awards-presidential-medal-freedom-stevie-wonder-meryl-streep-tom-brokaw-16-others ; Wikipedia (Oscar 1985, "I Just Called to Say I Love You")
- Presidential Medal of Freedom, 24th of November 2014: https://www.npr.org/2014/11/24/366379348/obama-awards-presidential-medal-of-freedom-to-18-people
