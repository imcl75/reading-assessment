## Changed (6 tests)
- 24-6 Mum's Saturday Secret: "at one o'clock" became "after our picnic". Clock time replaced by an event the child can picture. No question depended on it.
- 25-1 The Final Leg: "you nailed it" became "you did it brilliantly"; "by no more than a whisker" became "by only the tiniest bit". Both idioms could be misread. No question or guidance quoted them.
- 25-3 A Day at a Victorian School: "half past eight" became "early in the morning"; "four o'clock" became "late afternoon". True/False item 1 in Q10 rewritten to match (answer still True).
- 27-3 The Attic at Nana Joy's: "1968" on the photograph became "aged nineteen". True/False item 2 in Q9 rewritten as "The photograph had a name written on the back" (answer still True). Q6 clue (name Joy, same nose) is unaffected.
- 27-4 Wind Farm: "B&Bs" became "guest houses"; Q8 wording updated.
- 27-5 Penicillin: the quoted "That's funny" (a child may read it as humorous) became "That is strange"; Q10 quotation updated. Q10's point ("is said to have") is unchanged.

## Left alone, and why
- Real historical dates in genuine history texts (23-6 Anning, 25-3 the 1880s and 1891, 26-6 Braille, 27-5 Fleming): allowed by the brief, and several questions test them.
- 24-3 "Voyager 2 was launched in 1977": a science fact used in a True/False item; a year here is a fact, not a clock cue, so kept (could become "nearly fifty years ago" if wanted).
- Durations (26-1 "twenty minutes", 27-1 "an hour", 27-2 "five minutes"): children meet these in everyday life and 26-1 Q1 tests one.
- Richer vocabulary that sets the level: tarpaulin, quay, bollard, moorings, shrewd, bounty, fjords, presided, atria/ventricles, turbines, and so on. Challenging, not unfriendly, and several are tested by vocabulary questions.
- Tested idioms (tagged along, slept like a log, dug in her heels, weigh up, give way, this is the life): understandable in context, and some are the point of a question.
- UK usage is already consistent (Mum, flat, lolly stick, wellingtons, trolleys, digger, Brownies). "Yard" in 25-3 is normal UK school usage. No Americanisms found.
- Gender-balance fixes untouched.
- All other tests (23-1 to 23-5, 24-1 to 24-5, 25-2, 25-4 to 25-6, 26-1 to 26-5, 27-1, 27-2, 27-6): no problems found.
