## Patterns found
- Fearful girl comforted by a brave boy (18-2: Amara scared, Tomasz the rescuer).
- Fearful girl versus clever boy detective (22-3: Ellie "whispered nervously", Ravi solves it).
- Mum as default carer/caller (18-1 Mum calls the children in for hot chocolate).
